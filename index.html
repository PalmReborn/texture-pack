27 armor sets, plus the Diamond Hook and enchant runes

How to install

1

Download

Press the Download button. You get a file called Cobra Kitmap.zip. Do not unzip it.

2

Open the pack folder

In Minecraft go to Options, then Resource Packs, then Open Resource Pack Folder. Drop the zip in.

3

Turn it on

Back on the Resource Packs screen, move Cobra Kitmap to the right side and press Done.

You need OptiFine. Lunar Client and Badlion Client already have it built in. Joining the server also offers the pack automatically, and /pack in game gives you the download link.
